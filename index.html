A note from the founder

“
I built TaskHitch because I needed a better way to keep complex work moving with people who did not all participate through the same daily workflow.

When work involves several people and a lot of moving parts, the details should not be scattered across an inbox, a spreadsheet, and a meeting agenda.

TaskHitch makes the essential work easy to see, keeps deeper structure ready when it is needed, and keeps responsibility, updates, and decisions connected to the workflow.

Jason BurnsFounder, TaskHitch
